Connected accounts

Connect your social accounts for direct, free multi-platform broadcasting.

Setup notes
  • LinkedIn (Direct & 100% Free): Uses your official LinkedIn Developer App (w_member_social).
  • Telegram Channel (Free): Add TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and TELEGRAM_CHANNEL_ID to .env.
  • Discord (Free): Add DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL to .env.
  • X / Twitter (Free): Add TWITTER_API_KEY and tokens to .env.

Gmail (mail & expenses)

Connect Gmail (read-only) to fetch recent mail and auto-extract income & expenses on the dashboard.

Background poll to refresh mail & expenses. Minimum 1 hour; set 0 to turn off.

Setup notes
  • Add GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID and GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET to .env (see .env.example).
  • In Google Cloud, enable the Gmail API and add redirect URI http://localhost:3950/api/gmail/callback.
  • Scope used is gmail.readonly only.
